### **5. Conclusion — When should AI be used (or not)?**

Overall, Claude did a surprisingly good job with the first checklist, and I’d say good prompt engineering was the main reason why. By clearly laying out what we needed, the AI was able to get on the right track pretty quickly. That said, you can't expect AI to catch everything. With so many UI requirements to cover, a few items slipped through the cracks, so we still had to jump in and tweak things. Still, it saved us tons of time, and I'll for sure re-use this prompting style for upcoming tasks.
